## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: Houston homeowners** with a plumbing problem: a leak, a repair, a remodel, a re-pipe, a water heater. Many arrive from the r/houston thread where the family was recommended. Usually they're stressed, often on a phone, and want to know quickly whether these are honest people who will show up, and how to reach a real person.

**Secondary: commercial general contractors and developers** who bid ground-up new construction (restaurants, dealerships, c-stores, industrial). They judge on schedule reliability, blueprint competence, credentials and past projects. Commercial is the firm's deepest expertise, but on the site it mainly works as proof of quality for the homeowner.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for Texas Divine Plumbing, a family-owned plumbing company based in Houston that serves Texas. Its job is to turn a visitor into a call, a text or a contact-form inquiry to a named family member. Success means homeowners reach out directly, and GCs ask for bids.

## Positioning

A real family of plumbers, not a franchise or a call center. Trino has 40 years in Houston, Hector is a Licensed Master Plumber, Alexis (Rice MBA) runs bids and procurement, and seven uncles are also plumbers, all taught by Trino. Customers talk directly to the people who do the work. The crew that builds commercial kitchens for Whataburger also comes to your house. They work out of the house, so overhead is low and the savings go to the customer. They've had the same phone number for 40 years.

## Product Principles

1. **A real person is one tap away.** Every page makes calling or texting a named family member effortless, especially on mobile.
2. **Proof over promises.** Lead with real photos, real projects, real credentials and the real Reddit thread, never generic claims.
3. **Commercial rigor shown to homeowners.** Use the commercial track record to earn homeowner trust without losing GCs.
4. **Honest and plain.** Write the way the family talks. No inflated claims, no new guarantees.
