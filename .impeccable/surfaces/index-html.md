# Surface brief: homepage (index.html) and site-wide shell

Scope: index.html first; city pages, faq.html, blog.html and blog/ inherit the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade (homepage, city pages); Read (FAQ, blog articles).

Audience and job: Houston homeowner with a plumbing problem, often from r/houston, on a phone; secondary GCs checking credentials. Action: call or text Alexis (primary contact for every lead), or send the form to Alexis; Hector and Trino secondary. Proof: Reddit post, real job photos, four commercial builds, Google 5.0/47, real reviews, Master Plumber license, same number 40 years. Guardrail: must never read rough or cheap.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the family's group text opened to the customer ("I'll drop it in the family group chat"). Refuses the contractor template: hero + badge row + six icon cards + avatar testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched logo-blue wallpaper with a faint authored fittings pattern; white message sheets with tight corners and a notch; per-person color owns everything they touch: Trino copper, Hector PVC/inspection green, Alexis gold; visitor bubbles pale sky. Archivo across widths: expanded black for pinned headlines, normal for messages, condensed caps for timestamps and day separators; tabular numerals for every number.

STORY: Visitor lands in the thread, reads the family in their own words, sees the work as photo messages and the builds as albums, reads reviews as forwarded messages, and texts or calls a named person from the composer or the group-info panel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chat header bar (logo, "Texas Divine Plumbing", members, call). Desktop: thread column ~60% with a giant pinned headline then Alexis's Reddit message and a Hector photo message; right: sticky group-info panel with three tap-to-call members and the proof cross-check. Mobile: header, pinned headline, first messages, sticky composer bar (Call / Text) at the bottom.

FORM: Family group chat, candidate 6 of 7. Seed 64cafebc. Signature interaction: the composer picks a person and opens a prefilled text or sends the Netlify form; messages arrive with a brief typing indicator as they scroll in. Raises: one-fact-per-proof cross-check (six-pack); strict thread grid (ASCII); tabular spec numerals (forge); color ownership per person (rain garden).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
